Generic Optimizer Engine

A program search engine: you provide (1) what to optimize and (2) a benchmark; it returns the best program it found.

How it works

  • Proposer: Free LLM (Nemotron 3 Ultra via NVIDIA NIM) proposes program structures with diff_based_evolution: false
  • Bayesian tuning: Numeric constants marked TUNABLE(name, low, high) are optimized by Optuna (GPSampler if available, else TPE) before scoring
  • Testbed: 4 BBOB functions, 4 TSPLIB instances (elkai reference), 1 synthetic market with planted signal
  • Ablation arms: Fixed best style, Random, Thompson sampling, Thompson+Pair — 9 problems × 4 seeds × 4 arms × 40 proposals
  • Certificate: Multi-search agreement with stagnation detection, radical-round check, and planted-signal grading
